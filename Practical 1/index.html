About the Department

Established in 2001, the Department of Computer Science & Engineering at Redwood Institute is committed to excellence in teaching, innovation, and research. We nurture future-ready engineers through practical learning, interdisciplinary collaboration, and a strong focus on emerging technologies such as web development, data science, and software engineering.
